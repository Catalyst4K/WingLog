# Introduction

WingLog is a free companion app for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. It keeps your fleet, plans
flights with SimBrief, tracks them live from the sim, and keeps a logbook with a proper landing
report for every touchdown. It also works alongside two popular add-ons, GSX Pro and BeyondATC, if
you have them.

> **For flight simulation use only.** WingLog must never be used for real-world navigation,
> flight planning, or in a real aircraft.

## What WingLog does

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Fleet** | Your aircraft: registration, type, airline, photo, SimBrief profile, hours, flights, landings, and where each one last parked. |
| **Dispatch** | Plan a flight with SimBrief, check the weather, choose your departure and arrival procedures, then press **Fly**. |
| **Track** | The live flight: map, phase, times, procedures, weather, taxi routes, and automatic start and finish. |
| **Ground services** | GSX Pro's menu and services, run from inside WingLog (when turned on). |
| **BeyondATC** | BeyondATC's clearances, radio calls, actions and frequencies, plus automatic step climbs (when turned on). |
| **Logbook** | Every flight and every landing, with charts, a map of the route flown and a 0-100 landing score. |
| **Settings** | Units, language, theme, add-ons, tracking, your data, updates, and About. |

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 for live tracking. Fleet, Dispatch and the Logbook work without
  the sim running.
- A free SimBrief account for flight planning.
- Optional: GSX Pro, BeyondATC.

## Your data stays on your PC

Your fleet and logbook live in a database on your own computer. WingLog only goes online for the
features that need it:

| What | When | What's sent |
|---|---|---|
| SimBrief / Navigraph | Planning or fetching a flight, logging in | Your SimBrief username or login, through WingLog's own login service |
| Weather | Showing METARs | Airport codes |
| Map | Showing the map | The map area you're looking at |
| Aircraft lookup | Pressing **Look up** in Fleet | The registration |
| Exchange rates | Showing GSX invoices in your currency | The currencies |
| Airline logos | Showing an airline | The airline code |
| Update check | Shortly after launch and every few hours, if switched on | A request for the latest version number only |

There are no analytics, no tracking and no adverts.
